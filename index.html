⚡ HOW TO PLAY TAP RUSH

OBJECTIVE

You have exactly 30 seconds. Tap moving neon targets to score points, build combos, and climb the leaderboard. Target speed increases as time winds down!

TARGET ROSTER

Normal Orb (+100)
Standard moving target. Keep hitting to multiply combo!
Speedster (+250)
Smaller, fast zig-zagging target. Requires quick reflexes.
Golden Star (+500)
Rare bonus! Massive points + chance for +2 bonus seconds.
Ice Orb (+200)
Slows all target movements for 3.5 seconds. Rapid tap time!
Multi-Tap Core (+600)
Requires 3 rapid taps to shatter. Huge particle blast!
Hazard Bomb (-300)
DO NOT TAP! Deducts 300 pts, breaks combo & shakes arena!

PRO TIPS

  • 🔥 Don't tap empty space! Every missed tap resets your combo and lowers your accuracy rating.
  • ⚡ Combos multiply points up to 5x! Consecutive hits skyrocket your final score.
  • 📅 Daily Challenge uses a deterministic daily seed so you compete fairly against friends!
